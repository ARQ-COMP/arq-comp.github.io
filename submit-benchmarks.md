# Call for problems/<wbr>benchmarks/<wbr>comments

A competition of tools for quantum is being set up, and its shape is still
open. This call gathers the input needed to define it.

**[Send your input through the form]({{ site.form_url }})** — the deadline is
**30 September 2026**.

## What the call is asking for

### Problems

Which problems the competition should cover. Simulation of quantum circuits is
the natural candidate, but equivalence checking, verification, optimisation and
synthesis are all in scope — and so are problems from adjacent fields, if you
think they belong. Pointers to relevant papers are welcome.

### Benchmarks

Existing benchmark sets worth using, along with where they come from, what
format they are in, and how they can be obtained.

### Preliminary participation

Whether a tool of yours might take part. This is not a commitment. It is what
makes it possible to estimate how many participants to expect, what hardware
the competition will need, and which formats it has to support — so say so even
if you are only considering it.

The form also asks about non-standard hardware requirements, and leaves room
for any other opinions and proposals.
